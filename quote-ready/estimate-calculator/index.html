Markup is added to your cost. Margin is the gross profit as a percentage of the selling price. This calculator is for estimating purposes; taxes, overhead, travel, fees, and other job costs may need to be added separately.

Markup and margin are not the same

A 25% markup on $800 of cost adds $200, giving a $1,000 selling price. That $200 profit is a 20% gross margin on the selling price.
