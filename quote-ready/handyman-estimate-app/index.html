A practical handyman estimate checklist

Before you send a price, make sure the estimate tells the customer what is included and gives you enough detail to avoid confusion later.

1

Customer and job details

Record who the estimate is for and enough project detail to identify the work clearly.

2

Labor and materials

Break the job into understandable line items with quantities and prices instead of relying on one unexplained total.

3

Scope and notes

Write down important assumptions, exclusions, or job-specific notes while the details are fresh.

4

Tax and total

Check the numbers before sending so the customer can see the expected total clearly.

5

A customer-ready document

Send a clean PDF that is easier for the customer to review and keep than a price buried in a text message.

6

Follow the job through

When the work is approved, keep the quote and invoice workflow together instead of rebuilding the same information.
