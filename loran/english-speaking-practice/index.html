Try a simple exercise

What does real-life English speaking practice look like?

These are illustrative practice examples, not transcripts of a particular LORAN lesson. Say each response aloud, then try a different version.

Situation: Ordering at a café

Prompt: “What would you like to order?”

Your turn: “Could I have a small coffee, please?”

Try again: “I'd like an iced coffee without sugar, please.”

Situation: Making plans

Prompt: “Are you free this weekend?”

Your turn: “I'm free on Saturday afternoon. Would you like to meet?”

Try again: “Sunday works better for me. How about lunch?”

Picture description

Imagine a photo of a busy park. Describe what people are doing, where they are, and what might happen next.

Try: “Two people are walking along the path while a child plays near a tree.”

Practice with pictures

Turn what you see into spoken English.

Picture Practice gives you a visual starting point, so you can focus on expressing your own observations instead of simply repeating a written answer.

Start with one sentence, then add details about actions, places or feelings.

Make it a habit

Practice useful English expressions regularly.

Daily Expressions helps you focus on language you can actually use. LORAN also offers guided scenarios, speaking feedback and a hands-free practice flow.

  1. Choose English as your speaking practice language.
  2. Pick a real-life scenario, daily expression or picture exercise.
  3. Listen, speak aloud and practice responding in your own words.
  4. Return regularly to build confidence through repetition.
Frequently asked questions

About practicing English with LORAN

Can I practice speaking English by myself?

Yes. LORAN is designed for individual speaking practice, including guided scenarios, daily expressions and picture descriptions.

Does LORAN only teach English?

No. LORAN supports English, Mandarin Chinese, Spanish (Spain), Italian, French and Japanese speaking practice.

Can I practice without typing every response?

LORAN includes hands-free speaking practice with automatic listening, so you can focus on speaking aloud.

Is LORAN available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. You can find LORAN on Google Play and the Apple App Store using the links on this page.
