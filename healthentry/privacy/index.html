HealthEntry Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

HealthEntry is designed to let you quickly enter health information into Android Health Connect and view simple trends from health data you choose to share with the app.

Health Connect data

With your permission, HealthEntry may write the following data to Health Connect:

  • Hydration
  • Nutrition data used for caffeine entries
  • Weight
  • Blood pressure
  • Exercise sessions

With your permission, HealthEntry may also read these data types from Health Connect to display Trends. Trends can include records written by HealthEntry and records from other apps that you have chosen to make available through Health Connect.

How your health data is used

HealthEntry uses Health Connect data only to provide the app's health-entry and Trends features. The app does not use Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we do not sell your health data.

Storage and transmission

HealthEntry does not maintain a separate server-side history of your Health Connect health records. Health data read for Trends is used to display the requested trends in the app and is not uploaded to a HealthEntry server.

Permissions and control

You control which Health Connect permissions HealthEntry receives. You can review or revoke those permissions at any time in Health Connect. Some longer-range Trends, such as 90-day history, may require additional Health Connect history access where supported by your device.

Data deletion

Because HealthEntry does not keep a separate server-side copy of your Health Connect history, management and deletion of Health Connect records is handled through Health Connect and the apps that created those records. Revoking HealthEntry's permissions stops HealthEntry from accessing the affected Health Connect data.

Children

HealthEntry is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a HealthEntry server.

Changes to this policy

If HealthEntry's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

For privacy questions about HealthEntry, contact Clean Utility Apps through the contact information provided on the Clean Utility Apps website.
