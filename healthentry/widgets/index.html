Add Health Entry widgets

Log common health entries from your Android Home Screen without first opening Health Entry.

Water
Coffee
Weight
Exercise

Fastest way: add from Health Entry

  1. Open Health Entry.
  2. Use the widget card near the top of Home and choose Water, Coffee, Weight, or Exercise.
  3. Confirm the Android system prompt to place the widget on your Home Screen.

If your launcher does not support in-app adding

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Android Home Screen.
  2. Tap Widgets.
  3. Find Health Entry.
  4. Choose the widget you want and place it on your Home Screen.

Customize your widgets

Open Health Entry → Settings to choose the Water amount, Coffee default, and Exercise duration/name used by their widgets. Weight asks for a value when you use it.

Android launchers can differ slightly. If the in-app Add Widget request is unavailable, use your launcher's Widgets screen instead.
