Room-by-room move-in checklist

Photograph the overall room first, then capture close-ups of anything already scratched, stained, cracked, broken, missing, or worn.

Entry & living areas

  • Doors, locks and keys
  • Walls, paint and trim
  • Floors or carpet
  • Windows, screens and blinds
  • Lights, outlets and fixtures

Kitchen

  • Counters and cabinets
  • Sink and faucet
  • Refrigerator and freezer
  • Range, oven and microwave
  • Dishwasher and flooring

Bathroom

  • Sink, vanity and mirror
  • Toilet
  • Tub or shower
  • Tile, grout and caulk
  • Leaks, stains or water damage

Bedrooms

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Flooring
  • Closets and doors
  • Windows and coverings
  • Lights and outlets

Appliances & utilities

  • Visible appliance condition
  • Heating or cooling controls
  • Smoke/CO detectors
  • Laundry equipment if provided
  • Existing leaks or warning lights

Exterior or balcony

  • Door and threshold
  • Railings and flooring
  • Patio or balcony condition
  • Storage areas
  • Visible existing damage

Make the record useful later

Take wide shots and close-ups

A wide photo gives context. A close-up records the specific scratch, stain, crack, or other condition you want to remember.

Add a short factual note

Describe what you can see and where it is. Specific notes are easier to understand months later than a photo with no context.

Keep everything organized by room

Rental Inspect keeps photos and notes with the room and inspection instead of leaving you to reconstruct the walkthrough from a camera roll.

Turn the walkthrough into one PDF record

Rental Inspect lets renters, landlords, and property managers document conditions room by room and generate a PDF report with photos and notes. It is a documentation tool, not legal advice and does not guarantee any security-deposit outcome.
