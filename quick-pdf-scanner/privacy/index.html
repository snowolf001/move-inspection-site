Quick PDF Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Clean Utility Apps LLC provides Quick PDF Scanner. This policy explains privacy considerations for the app.

Documents and permissions

When you use document scanning features, the app may need access to your device camera and to documents or storage you choose to use. Only grant permissions you are comfortable with. You can review permissions in Android settings.

Document privacy

Scanned documents can contain sensitive personal information. Review what you scan, save, and share, and choose trusted destinations. If you share a PDF using another app or service, that provider's privacy practices apply.

Data handling

We do not require a Quick PDF Scanner account. The app is offered free of charge. This page does not claim that third-party platform services never process technical information; Android and app distribution services may process information under their own policies.

Deletion

You can manage documents saved on your device using Android's file management tools. Uninstalling the app may remove app-specific local data but may not delete files you exported elsewhere.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. For privacy questions, email contact@cleanutilityapps.com.
