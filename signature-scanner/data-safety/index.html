Data Safety

Current app implementation — November 18, 2026. This page summarizes the current app's privacy behavior. The Google Play Data Safety form must be reviewed against the exact release AAB before submission.

On-device processing

Signature photos, extracted transparent images, saved signature labels, and PDF documents are processed locally. We do not upload these documents to our servers.

Current collection and sharing

The app sends limited pseudonymous app activity analytics to our backend over HTTPS. Records include randomly generated installation, session, and event IDs; event names and timestamps; platform and app version; and predefined feature categories for signature and PDF operations. These data are used for usage measurement, troubleshooting and operational reporting. No account or advertising tracking SDK is required. We do not collect signature images, saved signature names, PDF contents, file paths, or document text through analytics, and we do not sell analytics data to advertisers.

User-controlled exports

If you export or share a signed PDF, you select the destination. Data transferred to another app, cloud storage provider, or recipient at your direction is subject to that destination's practices.

Local storage

Saved signatures are held in app-private storage. The Android app is configured to disable app-data backup. Users can delete saved signatures within the app.

Retention and deletion

Usage events may remain in active backend storage or operational archives without a fixed retention period. Requests to review or delete identifiable records can be sent to the contact below. Uninstalling the app does not remove previously received analytics events.

Google Play disclosure

Google Play Data Safety answers must disclose applicable app activity and pseudonymous identifiers collected by the released build, even though signature and PDF content stays on the device. Review the exact signed release AAB and backend data practices before submitting the form.
