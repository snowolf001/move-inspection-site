Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy applies to Signature Scanner, provided by Clean Utility Apps LLC.

What the app processes

You may choose or capture photos of signatures, save transparent signatures, and import and export PDF documents. Signature images, saved signature names and PDF contents are processed on your device; they are not uploaded to our servers for processing.

Data collection

The app collects limited pseudonymous usage analytics to measure app sessions and core actions, including signature extraction and saving, PDF import, PDF saving, and opening/closing the system PDF share sheet. Analytics records include a randomly generated app-install identifier, a session identifier, event identifier, event type, event timestamp, Android platform, app version, and a predefined feature category. These identifiers are not derived from your name or device hardware ID. Analytics are transmitted over HTTPS to our backend and may be retained for operational reporting and troubleshooting. The app does not send signature images, signature names, PDF contents, file paths, or document text in analytics events. No account is required, and the app does not use advertising tracking SDKs.

Analytics retention and requests

Analytics records may be retained in active storage and operational archives; we do not currently promise a fixed deletion period. You can contact us to ask about access or deletion of records we can reasonably identify. Because analytics use random identifiers rather than user accounts, we may not be able to associate a record with a particular person without additional information. Uninstalling the app removes its local analytics identifier but does not automatically delete events already received by our backend.

Storage and deletion

Saved signatures are stored in the app's private local storage. You can delete saved signatures in the app. Removing the app generally removes its private local data, subject to operating-system behavior. Files you explicitly export or share may remain in the destination you chose and are not removed by uninstalling the app.

Permissions and external services

Internet access is used to send limited usage analytics when configured, but signature extraction and PDF processing remain on-device. The camera is accessed only when you choose to take a photo. Photos and PDF files are selected using system interfaces. Sharing or saving an exported PDF uses the operating system or a destination you choose; the destination's privacy practices may apply. The Android app declares internet permission, but its signature and PDF workflows do not require uploading your documents.

Children

The app is a general-purpose document utility and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the app's behavior changes. For privacy questions contact contact@cleanutilityapps.com.
