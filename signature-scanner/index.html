Signature Scanner

Make a clean, transparent signature from a photo and add it to a PDF — privately, on your device.

What you can do

  • Take a photo or choose an existing signature photo.
  • Remove the paper background and adjust ink sensitivity.
  • Straighten, rotate, mirror and zoom the signature.
  • Save and manage multiple signatures privately on your device.
  • Import a PDF, position and resize the signature, and export a signed copy.

Privacy-first by design

Signature images and PDF contents are processed locally. The app does not require an account or upload your documents to our servers.
